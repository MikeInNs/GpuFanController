# Code of conduct

Keep issues, pull requests and discussions respectful and relevant to the project.

- Critique the code or idea, not the person.
- Do not harass, threaten, insult or discriminate against others.
- Do not post spam, credentials or someone else's private information.
- Respect the maintainer's time, decisions and lack of a response.

Participation does not entitle anyone to support, a review, a merge or an
explanation for every decision. The maintainer may close or remove disruptive
content and restrict participation. This is a small personal project, not a
moderated support service; continuous monitoring or a response is not promised.

Use GitHub's reporting tools for platform abuse. Please do not repeat harmful
content or private information in a new public issue.
